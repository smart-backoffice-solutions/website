Why “Backoffice”?

It started with a mortgage backoffice that could work smarter.

The company grew out of an information-extraction pipeline for mortgage documents. It helps advisers prepare applications, and lenders check and accept them.

The same combination of knowledge turned out to be useful well beyond mortgages.

Domain knowledge matters

Good solutions start with understanding the problem.

What we build is only as good as our grasp of the substance behind it. We use data to establish what can actually be measured and defended.

The work has academic roots: one of us is a full professor at Tilburg University.

Recent work

Two examples

One is infrastructure that other software talks to. The other is an app you hold in your hand.

Mortgage Documents

An extraction pipeline for the documents in a mortgage application: payslips, employer declarations, identity documents, purchase agreements, appraisal reports, DUO statements and Chamber of Commerce extracts. A document goes in; every relevant field comes back as structured data, each with a confidence score. Advisers work faster, lenders get what they need for their checks, and the complexity stays out of sight.
