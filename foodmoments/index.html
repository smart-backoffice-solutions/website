Nutrition beyond calories

Calories are only the beginning.

Food is not just a number. It is energy, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fibre, timing and context. Food Moments shows where you stand over the day — so you can see what makes sense next before the next meal, not after it.

Your personal feed

One meal is a number. A day of Food Moments is a story.

Your meals, drinks and snacks appear naturally over the course of the day — photos first, like a personal feed viewed through nutrition. Seeing food chronologically makes patterns visible: what breakfast looked like, how much protein came before dinner, how balanced the day became.

Have the dessert. Enjoy the pizza. The point is to know what it means — and make the next decision with better information.

What we stand for

Eat with context, not guilt.

Nobody eats perfectly all the time — and nobody should have to. Food Moments is for people who care about themselves, not for people being punished for eating.

Private by design

Your food. Your data.

No account, no cloud profile. Your meals, photos and history stay on your iPhone.

🔒

Everything is stored on your phone. Photos and notes are sent out only for the nutritional analysis you ask for — with your consent, and never to build a profile. The details are in the privacy policy, in plain language.
