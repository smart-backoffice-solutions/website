Food Moments — Support & Privacy

Food Moments estimates the nutritional value of your meals from photos and your own words — with no account, and all data stored on your iPhone.

Get help: email us at info@smart-backoffice.nl. We reply within a few working days.

Documents

  • Supplemental Terms — the app is licensed under Apple's standard EULA

Your data. Food Moments stores your meals only on your iPhone. To export everything, use Settings → Backup in the app. To delete everything, delete meals in the app or delete the app itself. To withdraw your consent to AI analysis, use Settings → Privacy. Because we store nothing on servers, there is nothing we could access, correct or delete for you — but for any privacy question or request (access, correction, deletion), email info@smart-backoffice.nl and we will answer within one month.

Where your data is processed. A meal analysis runs on our Microsoft Azure deployment in the EU Data Zone. Online dictation is the one exception: its speech deployment is a global one, so a dictation recording may be processed outside the EU, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in §5 of the Privacy Policy. Type your note instead of dictating, and nothing about it leaves your iPhone.

If we ever stop. Food Moments comes from a small company, so §6 of the Supplemental Terms says plainly what happens if we one day stop developing it: a subscription you paid for runs to its end, we announce it first, your backup keeps working without us, and we intend to offer an export that lets you keep browsing your past meal moments without the app.
