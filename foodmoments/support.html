Food Moments — Support & Privacy

Food Moments estimates the nutritional value of your meals from photos and your own words — with an account that is only your email address, and your data stored in the European Union.

Get help: email us at info@smart-backoffice.nl. We reply within a few working days.

Documents

  • Privacy Policy
  • Supplemental Terms — the app is licensed under Apple's standard EULA

Your data. Your meals live in your account, on our servers in the European Union; your iPhone keeps the recent days. To take everything with you, use Settings → Account → Download all my data. To delete everything, use Settings → Account → Delete account; individual meals are deleted in the app. To withdraw your consent to AI analysis or dictation, use Settings → Privacy. For any privacy question or request (access, correction, deletion), email info@smart-backoffice.nl and we will answer within one month.

A new iPhone? Install Food Moments and sign in with your email address — a six-digit code arrives by email, no password to remember — and your meals, photos and profile come back by themselves.

Downloading all your data. Settings → Account → Download all my data has the server pack everything into one zip — every photo, a set of pages you can browse on any computer, and a table for a spreadsheet — and emails you a download link. The link works for 24 hours; the file is deleted from our server within 48.

Deleting your account. Settings → Account → Delete account erases everything we hold — meals, photos, profile, sessions, any waiting export — immediately and irreversibly. Download your data first if you want to keep it.

Where your data is processed. Your account's data is stored on Microsoft Azure in the European Union (West Europe), and a meal analysis runs on our Azure deployment in the EU Data Zone. Online dictation is the one exception: its speech deployment is a global one, so a dictation recording may be processed outside the EU, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in §5 of the Privacy Policy. Type your note instead of dictating, and nothing about it leaves the European Union.

If we ever stop. Food Moments comes from a small company, so §6 of the Supplemental Terms says plainly what happens if we one day stop developing it: a subscription you paid for runs to its end, we announce it first, and Download all my data gives you an export you can keep browsing without the app.

Changes to the privacy policy

30 August 2026 — Crash diagnostics, and the on-device usage statistics are gone. If the app crashes or freezes, Apple's own on-device detection (MetricKit) sends us a technical report — what kind of failure it was, your iPhone model and iOS version, the app version — so we can find and fix it; it carries none of your photos, notes or anything you typed, is kept 90 days and then deleted automatically, and is tied to your iPhone's installation, not your account. Separately, the Usage card that used to show AI cost statistics in Settings, and the local records behind it, have been removed — what a call costs is now answered from our own server-side ledger, not tracked on your phone. See §§2, 4 and 6 of the Privacy Policy.

29 August 2026 — Apple Health, stated precisely; backup references removed. If you take your daily targets from Apple Health, each day's computed energy target — together with the burned- calories figure behind it — is stored with that day in your account, like the rest of your timeline; the Health readings themselves stay on your iPhone and are never sent to the AI. §2 of the privacy policy now says this explicitly. References to the retired in-app backup were removed from the policy, the terms and this page — Download all my data is the way to take everything with you.

29 August 2026 — Your meals move into an account of your own. Food Moments now has accounts: your email address plus a one-time code — no password, no name. Your meals, photos and profile are stored in that account on our servers in the European Union, so a new iPhone brings everything back and deleting the app no longer deletes your data. Settings → Account adds the two buttons that go with that: Download all my data and Delete account. Fair use is now counted per account instead of per installation. See §§2, 6, 7 and 8 of the Privacy Policy.

28 August 2026 — A gateway of our own. Every analysis and dictation request now passes through a small server we operate on Microsoft Azure in the European Union, instead of going straight to the AI service. It exists so the keys to that service are no longer inside the app, where anyone could extract them. It stores nothing you send — photos, notes and recordings pass through and are gone — and keeps only an anonymous identifier for your iPhone that proves a request comes from the real app. See §§2, 3 and 6 of the Privacy Policy.

Os seus dados. As suas refeições vivem na sua conta, nos nossos servidores na União Europeia; o seu iPhone mantém os dias recentes à mão. Para levar tudo consigo, use Definições → Conta → Descarregar todos os meus dados. Para apagar tudo, use Definições → Conta → Eliminar conta; as refeições individuais eliminam-se na aplicação. Para retirar o seu consentimento à análise por IA ou ao ditado, use Definições → Privacidade. Para qualquer questão ou pedido de privacidade (acesso, retificação, apagamento), escreva para info@smart-backoffice.nl e responderemos no prazo de um mês.

Um iPhone novo? Instale o Food Moments e inicie sessão com o seu endereço de e-mail — chega um código de seis dígitos por e-mail, sem palavra-passe para decorar — e as suas refeições, fotografias e perfil voltam por si.

Descarregar todos os seus dados. Definições → Conta → Descarregar todos os meus dados faz o servidor empacotar tudo num zip — cada fotografia, um conjunto de páginas para qualquer computador e uma tabela para uma folha de cálculo — e envia-lhe por e-mail uma ligação de descarga. A ligação funciona durante 24 horas; o ficheiro é eliminado do nosso servidor no prazo de 48.

Eliminar a sua conta. Definições → Conta → Eliminar conta apaga tudo o que temos — refeições, fotografias, perfil, sessões, qualquer exportação à espera — de imediato e de forma irreversível. Descarregue primeiro os seus dados se quiser conservá-los.

Onde os seus dados são tratados. Os dados da sua conta estão guardados na Microsoft Azure na União Europeia (Europa Ocidental), e uma análise de refeição corre na nossa implementação Azure na EU Data Zone. O ditado online é a única exceção: a sua implementação de voz é global, pelo que uma gravação de ditado pode ser tratada fora da UE, incluindo nos EUA, ao abrigo das garantias descritas no §5 da política de privacidade. Se escrever a nota em vez de a ditar, nada dela sai da União Europeia.

Se um dia pararmos. O Food Moments é feito por uma empresa pequena; o §6 das condições complementares diz com clareza o que acontece se um dia deixarmos de o desenvolver: uma subscrição paga corre até ao fim, anunciamo-lo primeiro, e «Descarregar todos os meus dados» dá-lhe uma exportação que pode continuar a folhear sem a aplicação.
