2. Estimates, not medical advice

Food Moments estimates the nutritional value of meals from photos and your descriptions using AI models. All values are estimates. Photo-based nutrition estimation has known error margins; published research puts typical energy estimates around a third off, and the app therefore shows a confidence level and makes corrections easy. Do not rely on the numbers where accuracy matters — verify important information yourself.

Food Moments provides general nutritional information only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical, dietary or diagnostic advice. Consult a qualified professional before making significant dietary changes, and always where health conditions (diabetes, allergies, eating disorders, pregnancy) are involved.

4. Fair use of the analysis service

The subscription covers your personal, non-commercial use. If usage goes far beyond what personal use plausibly needs, or appears automated or scripted, we may slow down or temporarily pause further analyses; the rest of the app keeps working and your data is untouched. 5. Your data

Your meals, photos and estimates are stored in your account, on our servers in the European Union; your iPhone keeps the recent days at hand and fetches older ones when you scroll back to them. Signing in on a new iPhone brings everything back, and deleting the app no longer loses anything. The data stays yours in the practical sense too: Settings → Account can hand you everything as one download, and Delete account erases everything we hold — irreversibly, so take the download first if you want to keep it. The one-tap backup in Settings still works and is still worth making: a file in your own hands depends on nobody, us included. How the account and its data are processed is described in the Privacy Policy.

6. Availability, changes — and what happens if we stop

Analysis depends on third-party AI services and an internet connection; we do not guarantee uninterrupted availability. We may change, add or retire features, and may switch the underlying AI models, maintaining comparable quality.

Food Moments is made by a small company, and we would rather say this plainly than leave it unsaid: it is possible that one day we stop developing Food Moments. If that happens, this is what you can count on.

  • A subscription you paid for runs to its end. We stop selling new subscriptions and turn off automatic renewal, so nobody pays for a period we do not intend to serve. A subscription already paid for keeps working — analysis included — until the period you paid for is over. Renewals, cancellations and refunds continue to be handled by Apple under Apple's terms.
  • Notice first. We announce the decision in the app and on the support page as early as we reasonably can, and in any case before the analysis service is switched off.
  • Your data reaches your hands before anything is switched off. Your meal moments live in your account (§5), so before that storage is switched off we say so — and until then both ways of taking everything with you keep working: Settings → Backup writes a complete copy — photos, meals, estimates, corrections — to a file you keep wherever you like, and Download all my data packs the same into a single download. A file you have saved needs neither us nor an internet connection afterwards.
  • An export you can actually open. This stood here as an intent; it is now built in: Settings → Account → Download all my data delivers every photo, a set of pages you can keep and browse on your own computer, and a table a spreadsheet can read — so your past meal moments stay viewable long after the app has stopped.
  • The app keeps working for a while. With the analysis service switched off, the app on your iPhone still shows your timeline and everything you have recorded; it simply stops producing new estimates. Once the storage behind your account follows — announced first, like everything else — the app shows what is on your iPhone. An app that is no longer maintained will eventually stop working on newer versions of iOS and will be removed from the App Store — which is why we would ask you to make your backup and your export while everything still runs.
